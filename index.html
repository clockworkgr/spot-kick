Beat the chain.
Take the pot.

Pay a game's entry fee, then shoot five and keep five against the chain. Beat it by the game's margin (1, 2 or 3 goals) and 80% of the pot is yours (20% goes to the game's creator); otherwise your fee stays in it for the next challenger.

Every kick is settled in the block it lands in: the chain's move and the mishit come from SHA-256 of the block time, and the realm runs the same physics engine as this page, bit for bit, so the 3-D replay is exactly what the chain decided. Running the physics on chain is heavy: each kick's fee covers a 0.7B gas limit (0.7 GNOT at gnodev prices) plus ~0.3 GNOT of storage.

Create a game

You seed the pot. Challengers pay the entry fee into it; the first to beat the chain by your chosen margin takes 80% of it, and you get the other 20%. The larger the margin, the more challengers the pot collects first, and the larger the pot you can seed. You can cancel for a refund until someone plays.
